Aspiring Web Developer


Welcome to my coding journey! I'm an aspiring web developer from Chelsea, Alabama, and a wife and mother of three amazing kids. You may be wondering, why tech? I've always been fascinated by technology and had a natural ability to learn new systems, but finding a career path I truly enjoyed wasn't always easy. Join me as I explore the exciting world of web development!

At 19, I became a mother and soon after, a wife. While I didn't finish college, I spent the years that followed building a career and, most importantly, raising my family. But I never stopped wondering what else I was capable of. Then one day, while scrolling through TikTok, I came across a video about CodeSquad. Something inside me said, “Apply. This is something you can do.” So I did. Since beginning this journey, I've loved everything about learning web development. I'm excited to continue growing my skills, building new things, and eventually contributing my own ideas to the tech community.
